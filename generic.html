My Story

Grew up in Zimbabwe, spending a lot of time trying to learn as much as I can and playing chess with my family and friends. I'm passionate about exploring the world beyond Zimbabwe's borders.

"My goal is not to wake up at forty with the bitter realization that I've wasted my life on a job I hated."

I've always loved numbers, which led me to pursue Financial Engineering. Through my studies, I discovered the power of data science and machine learning in generating insights and driving business decisions.

This realization motivated me to obtain certifications in data science, where I worked with real-world data from various institutions. My passion for data has grown so strong that I'm now pursuing a master's degree in Data Science.

I'm particularly interested in data management, cleaning, feature selection, and applying machine learning models to solve complex problems using tools like Python, R, and MATLAB.

Currently, I'm developing a VBA-based data entry system that automates loan application processes, significantly improving efficiency.

"Therefore, I want to follow my passion by doing what I believe in and learning every day to be the best in data field."
