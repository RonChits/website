About Me

Hello! My name is Ronald Chitsatso, and I am a passionate and dedicated full-stack developer with a strong focus on creating clean, efficient, and user-friendly web applications. My journey in tech began with a deep curiosity about how things work, and it quickly grew into a passion for building and creating.
