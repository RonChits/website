My Story

Hello! I'm Ronald Chitsatso, a recent graduate with a Bachelor of Technology Honors Degree in Financial Engineering from Harare Institute of Technology. My academic journey has instilled in me a deep appreciation for the power of data and numbers. I believe that behind every financial decision and market trend, there are underlying reasons and patterns waiting to be discovered through rigorous analysis.

Over the past few years, this belief has evolved into a strong passion for **data science**. I've dedicated myself to understanding how to extract meaningful insights from large datasets, clean messy information, and build predictive models that can drive informed decisions. My goal is to apply these skills in a challenging and rewarding career, where I can continue to learn and contribute to innovative solutions.

"My journey has always been guided by curiosity and a relentless pursuit of understanding the 'why' behind the numbers."

Beyond my formal education, I'm a proud **Applied Data Science lab holder at WorldQuant University**, an experience that has significantly enhanced my practical skills in various data science methodologies. This program pushed me to apply theoretical knowledge to real-world financial datasets, honing my abilities in areas like quantitative analysis, machine learning for financial forecasting, and risk management.

I'm constantly exploring new tools and techniques, staying updated with the latest advancements in data science and financial technology. My portfolio, as seen on the main page, showcases a range of projects from predicting loan defaults to analyzing market volatility, each reflecting my commitment to data-driven problem-solving.

Thank you for visiting my portfolio. I'm excited about the possibilities that data science offers and look forward to connecting with opportunities where I can make a tangible impact.
